Precision Application

Simple. Scientific. Guaranteed.

Our certified technicians perform every doorstep service using medical-grade micron atomizers and ISO-certified non-hazardous formulas.

01

Inspect & Deep Decontaminate

We analyze your surface material weave, pH levels, and pre-existing stains. A specialized dry vacuuming and fiber neutralizer removes micro-dust particles from deep within yarn pores.

02

Precision Molecular Atomization

Using low-pressure high-volume (HVLP) micron spray systems, our technicians apply an ultra-fine mist that bonds at the molecular level, wrapping each individual thread in a transparent silica cocoon.

03

Rapid Ambient Cross-Link Cure

The coating cross-links and hardens within 4 to 6 hours at room temperature. We conduct a live hydrophobic water bead drop test in front of you before presenting your official warranty certificate.
